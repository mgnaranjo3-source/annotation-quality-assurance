# Reviewer Feedback Templates

Good feedback is **specific, tied to the guideline, and respectful**. It explains what to do next time.

## Template
> **Item:** <id>
> **Your label:** <label> → **Expected:** <label>
> **Why:** <guideline section + one-sentence reason>
> **Tip for next time:** <concrete action>

## Examples

**Boundary miss**
> **Item:** G03
> **Your label:** POS → **Expected:** NEU
> **Why:** Section 2, boundary examples (v1.1): "meets expectations" without praise words is NEU.
> **Tip for next time:** Look for explicit praise words ("love", "great", "perfect") before choosing POS.

**Sarcasm miss**
> **Item:** G06
> **Your label:** POS → **Expected:** NEG
> **Why:** Section 4: label the intended meaning. "Great, another broken update" is a complaint.
> **Tip for next time:** When a positive word is followed by a negative outcome, check for sarcasm.

**Vague rationale (AI response rating)**
> **Your rationale:** "Response B is worse."
> **Why it needs work:** It doesn't say *what* is wrong or *where*.
> **Stronger version:** "Response B states the boiling point of water at sea level is 90 °C (correct: 100 °C). This factual error caps Accuracy at 2 and would mislead the user."

## Tone do's and don'ts
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "The guideline says…" | "You always…" |
| Point to the exact section | Say "this is wrong" with no reason |
| Give one clear tip | List ten minor issues at once |
| Acknowledge good work too | Only send feedback when there's a problem |
